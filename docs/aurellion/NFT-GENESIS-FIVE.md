# AURELLION — Connected Light: Genesis Five

**Status:** Art direction and digital collectible concept only. No original image has been rendered, no contract deployed, no NFTs minted, and no coin issued.

## Art direction

A luminous many-source composition: one warm golden beacon surrounded by five distinct orbital forms joined by fine cyan bridges. Each form retains its own silhouette and identity. The composition communicates cooperation without absorption.

Palette: gold (wisdom), cyan (clarity), emerald (growth), obsidian (humility before the unknown), saffron (creative energy). These are artistic associations, not scientific claims.

## Five works

1. **The Beacon of Reason** — discernment, humility, and clear thought.
2. **The Living Archive** — provenance, memory, and preservation of originals.
3. **The Bridge Between Worlds** — cooperation while retaining identity.
4. **The Open Hand of Creation** — creativity, consent, and shared opportunity.
5. **The Garden of the Greater Good** — outcomes that benefit people beyond the creators.

## Token standard research

- ERC-721 is a standard for individually unique non-fungible tokens.
- ERC-1155 supports multiple token types, including fungible and non-fungible patterns.
- EIP-2981 provides a royalty-information interface; it does not guarantee that every marketplace will pay royalties.

A digital collectible is not equity, a bank deposit, a guaranteed royalty, or proof of investment value. A collectible and a fungible coin are different instruments; this foundation defines no fungible coin.

## Release gates

Before any mint: complete trademark and copyright review; identify human creative contributions; confirm artwork and metadata rights; define token license separately from token ownership; complete tax, consumer-protection, and legal review; select chain, contract, supply, wallet custody, pricing, and marketplace only after review; commission independent smart-contract audit and testnet validation; obtain explicit approval for each irreversible on-chain action.

No financial return, market value, resale, or royalty payment is promised.
